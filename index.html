QUICK ANSWERS

Before you write.

Here are answers to a few common questions.

How do I adjust and stop the whistle?

On the Modulator screen, touch and drag across the dark surface to adjust the signal. Double-tap the surface to stop it. The session timer also stops the whistle when the selected time ends.

Why can’t I hear the whistle?

Some frequencies may be difficult for people to hear, and sound output varies between speakers and devices. Check your volume and audio output. Start at low intensity, keep sessions short and stop if your dog shows discomfort. Never test the sound close to an ear.

How do saved signals and the training diary work?

With Pro, save a selected signal and replay it from Signals. In the Training Diary, record your practice goal and the reaction you observed. The latest observation is available for free; Pro unlocks the full history. Entries stay on your device and reflect your observations, not a measurement of your dog’s hearing.

Why is Smart Trainer unavailable?

Smart Trainer needs iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, a compatible device, Apple Intelligence enabled and a supported language. Model availability can also depend on whether it is ready on your device. The Training Diary works without Apple Intelligence.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings, open the Pro screen and choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account used for the purchase. If you still need help, contact us without sending passwords or payment information.

How do clicker sounds and temporary access work?

The classic clicker is available for free. Pro unlocks all clicker variants. Where a rewarded option is offered, you can choose to watch an ad to unlock an individual clicker or the whistle for one hour.
