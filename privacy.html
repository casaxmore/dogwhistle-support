Last updated: November 25, 2026

This notice explains how José Manuel Casabona, operating as Casaxmore, handles information when you use the Dog Whistle Clicker support website or email us. Contact: casaxmore@gmail.com.

Contacting support

When you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and handle related support. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

We process app support requests to provide assistance related to the app; for general enquiries, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding. We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and comply with applicable obligations.

Website hosting and service providers

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP address, browser and request information for hosting and security. Email is handled through Gmail. These providers may process data in other countries under their own terms and applicable safeguards.

This website does not use its own analytics, advertising scripts or a contact form. The email button opens your mail application.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us at casaxmore@gmail.com.

The app

This notice covers the support website and support correspondence. The Dog Whistle Clicker app has its own separate privacy policy, including information about advertising, purchases and training data.
